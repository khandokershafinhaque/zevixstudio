How long does a typical project take?
Landing pages take 5–7 days. Full websites take 10–14 days. Web apps and e-commerce stores vary by scope. We'll give you a clear timeline before we begin.
Do you work with international clients?
Absolutely. We work with clients worldwide via email, WhatsApp, and video calls. Timezone differences are never an obstacle — we adapt to your schedule.
What do you need from me to get started?
Just your vision and basic information about your business. We handle the rest — including content guidance, design direction, and technical setup.
How many revisions are included?
Starter projects include 2 rounds, standard projects include 3 rounds, and larger builds are unlimited. We want you 100% satisfied — we'll make it right.
Will my website be mobile-friendly?
Every website we build is fully responsive — tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop before launch. Mobile-first is our default approach, not an afterthought.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance?
Yes. We offer monthly maintenance packages covering updates, security monitoring, performance checks, and minor content changes. Ask about our retainer options.
